What Fero is

Fero is a workout-accountability service for invited groups called Blocs. Members of a Bloc agree a monthly workout target, record the workouts they do, and can see each other's progress. Where a Bloc chooses to attach a forfeit to missing that target, Fero records what the Bloc has agreed and what each member owes.

Fero does not handle money

Fero does not process, hold, transfer, collect or refund any payment. Fero has no payment processing of any kind.

Where a Bloc agrees a forfeit, that agreement is between the members of that Bloc. Any amount shown in Fero is a record of what those members told Fero they agreed. Members settle with each other directly, by whatever means they choose, entirely outside Fero. A member may choose to save a payment handle so that other members know where to send money; that handle is information shown to their Bloc, not a payment instrument, and Fero does not use it to move money.

Marking an amount as paid, confirmed or disputed in Fero records what a member has said. It is a note in a shared ledger. It is not proof of payment, it does not settle anything, and Fero cannot verify it.

Fero is not a bank, a payment service, an escrow service, a money transmitter, or a party to any agreement between members. Fero does not guarantee that any member will pay, cannot recover money on anyone's behalf, and is not responsible for any amount a member does not pay. Disagreements about money are between the members concerned.
