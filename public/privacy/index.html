What Fero is

Fero is a workout-accountability service for invited groups called Blocs. This policy explains the information Fero needs to provide the service.

Information we may collect

  • Account information, including your email address and account identifiers.
  • Profile information, including your display name and profile photo.
  • Bloc membership, invitations, monthly targets, workout logs, dates, counts, notes, photos, comments, reactions, reports, blocks, and event responses.
  • Member-selected contact or payment-handle information if that feature is enabled in the final release.
  • Technical and usage information needed to keep the service secure and understand whether it works, subject to the final analytics configuration.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate members, operate private Blocs, show workout progress and history, store photos and messages, provide moderation and safety controls, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

Sharing and service providers

Fero uses hosting, authentication, database, storage, email, and other service providers needed to operate the service. The final policy will name the actual providers and explain the applicable data-processing and transfer arrangements.

Photos and user content

Members decide what workout and profile content they add to a Bloc. Content may be visible to members of the relevant Bloc. The final policy will explain moderation, reports, blocks, deletion, and what happens to shared historical content when a member leaves or deletes an account.

Workout photo retention

Workout photos are temporary. Fero's scheduled cleanup removes workout photos after they are more than approximately 72 hours old. Photos involved in a moderation decision may be retained for a shorter period after that decision. Profile photos are handled separately and are removed when you replace them or delete your account.

Retention and deletion

Members can request account deletion in the app. The final policy will state what is deleted, what may remain in other members' historical views, the retention period for security or legal records, and the expected completion time. These details must match the tested implementation.

Security

Fero uses access controls, authenticated requests, private photo delivery, and other safeguards appropriate to the service. No service can promise absolute security.

Contact and changes

Support email, legal publisher identity, contact address, effective date, and the method for notifying users of material changes will be added after founder and legal review.
