Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

What Fero is

Fero is a workout-accountability service for invited groups called Blocs. This policy explains the information Fero needs to provide the service.

Information we may collect

  • Account information, including your email address and account identifiers.
  • Profile information, including your display name and profile photo.
  • Bloc membership, invitations, monthly targets, workout logs, dates, counts, notes, photos, comments, reactions, reports, and event responses.
  • Member-selected contact or payment-handle information, where a member chooses to add it.
  • Technical and usage information needed to keep the service secure and understand how the service is used.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate members, operate private Blocs, show workout progress and history, store photos and messages, provide moderation and safety controls, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

Sharing and service providers

Fero uses Supabase for authentication, database, and storage; Vercel for API hosting and web delivery; Cloudflare for domain and inbound email routing; and Gmail for the monitored support inbox. These providers process information only as needed to operate, secure, and support the service.

Photos and user content

Members decide what workout and profile content they add to a Bloc. Content may be visible to members of the relevant Bloc. Reports can affect visibility and moderation. Account deletion removes the member's account profile and photos. Some shared historical records may remain visible to other Bloc members where necessary to preserve the Bloc's history.

Workout photo retention

Workout photos are temporary. Fero automatically removes workout photos from storage once they are more than about 72 hours old. A photo that has been reported may stop being shown sooner, once the report is decided. Profile photos are stored separately, are not removed on that schedule, and are deleted when you delete your account.

Retention and deletion

Members can request account deletion in the app. Account deletion removes the member's account profile and photos. Some shared historical records may remain visible to other Bloc members where necessary to preserve the Bloc's history. Fero may retain limited security, moderation, or legal records for as long as needed for those purposes.

Security

Fero uses access controls, authenticated requests, and other safeguards appropriate to the service. No service can promise absolute security.

Contact and changes

Fero is operated by Aadhil Shah Jahan. For privacy questions or requests, email support@joinfero.app. We may update this policy when the service or its data practices materially change and will update the effective date above.
