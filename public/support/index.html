Account deletion

To request account deletion in Fero, open Settings → Account → Delete account. The final page will state the actual deletion timeline and any historical content retained for other members.

Safety reports

Use the report and block controls in the app for content or member-safety concerns. For an urgent safety or security issue, contact the support address above once it is published.
