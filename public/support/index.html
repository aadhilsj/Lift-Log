Support

Support for Fero members

Fero support helps with sign-in, invitations, workout records, photos, moderation reports, account deletion, and suspected security problems.

Contact

Support email: support@joinfero.app

When writing, include the account email, Bloc name, device and iOS version, and a short description of the problem.

What not to send

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, identity documents, or payment credentials.

Account deletion

To request account deletion in Fero, open the Profile tab, tap your name, then choose Delete account. Account deletion removes the member's account profile and photos. Some shared historical records may remain visible to other Bloc members where necessary to preserve the Bloc's history.

Workout photo retention

Workout photos are temporary. Fero automatically removes workout photos from storage once they are more than about 72 hours old. A photo that has been reported may stop being shown sooner, once the report is decided. Profile photos are stored separately, are not removed on that schedule, and are deleted when you delete your account.

Safety reports

Use the report control on an opened workout photo in the app if something should not be there. For an urgent safety or security issue, contact support@joinfero.app.
