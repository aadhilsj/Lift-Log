# Solo rules — product decisions to carry forward

## Agreed new Solo rules

- Solo is for a heavier month: the member keeps logging with an automatic goal
  of 50% of the Bloc target.
- A member who reaches their Solo goal pays no penalty.
- A member who misses their Solo goal pays the standard base monthly penalty.
  This penalty is fixed even when the Bloc uses escalating fines, and a Solo
  miss does not increase anybody else's escalating fine.
- A Solo member cannot receive a Bloc reward for that month.
- Existing September 2026 Solo members keep the old rules for September. New
  Solo starts use the new rules once the implementation is ready.

## Deliberately unresolved: everybody misses

If no non-Solo member reaches their target, a Solo member who misses their own
target should **still pay the fixed Solo penalty**. The founder has further
ideas for how an all-members-miss month should work; do not invent or implement
that outcome yet. Revisit the full all-members-miss experience in a future
product-design session before changing settlement behaviour beyond the fixed
Solo penalty.

## Proposed Solo sheet copy

> **Go Solo for September?**
>
> Solo is for a heavier month — when you still want to keep showing up, but
> need a lighter goal.
>
> Your goal will be **6 workouts**, half of your Bloc's usual target. You'll
> keep logging as normal. Reach your Solo goal and you're clear. If you fall
> short, you pay the standard monthly penalty.
>
> Solo members can avoid a penalty by reaching their goal, but they can't
> receive a reward this month.
