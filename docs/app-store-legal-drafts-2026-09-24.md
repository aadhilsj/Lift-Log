## Privacy Policy — working draft

### What Fero is

Fero is a private workout-accountability service for invited groups called
Blocs. This draft describes the information Fero needs to provide the service.

### Information we may collect

- Account information, including email address and account identifiers.
- Profile information, including display name and profile photo.
- Bloc membership, invitations, monthly targets, workout logs, dates, counts,
  notes, photos, comments, reactions, reports, blocks, and event responses.
- Member-selected contact or payment-handle information if the feature is
  enabled in the final release.
- Technical and usage information needed to keep the service secure and
  understand whether it works, subject to the final analytics configuration.

### How we use it

We use this information to authenticate members, operate private Blocs, show
workout progress and history, store photos and messages, provide moderation
and safety controls, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain
the service.

### Sharing and service providers

Fero uses hosting, authentication, database, storage, email, and other service
providers needed to operate the service. The final published policy must name
the actual providers and explain the applicable data-processing and transfer
arrangements.

### Photos and user content

Members decide what workout and profile content they add to a Bloc. Content may
be visible to members of the relevant Bloc. The final policy must explain
moderation, reports, blocks, deletion, and what happens to shared historical
content when a member leaves or deletes an account.

### Retention and deletion

Members can request account deletion in the app. The final policy must state
what is deleted, what may remain in other members' historical views, the
retention period for security or legal records, and the expected completion
time. The policy must match the tested implementation.

### Security

Fero uses access controls, authenticated requests, private photo delivery, and
other safeguards appropriate to the service. No service can promise absolute
security. The final policy should describe the safeguards without exposing
secrets or internal infrastructure details.

## Terms of Use — working draft

Fero lets invited groups record workouts and accountability outcomes. Members
must provide accurate information, protect their login access, respect other
members, and only upload content they have the right to share.

Fero does not hold funds, transfer money, operate a prize pool, sell wagering
credit, or verify off-platform settlements. Any accountability obligation or
settlement status shown in Fero is a record between members, not a payment
service provided by Fero.

Users must not harass, threaten, impersonate, defraud, exploit, or upload
unlawful, abusive, private, or rights-infringing material. Fero may remove
content, restrict access, or suspend accounts when necessary for safety,
security, legal compliance, or service operation, subject to the final policy
and applicable law.

The final version must add the publisher identity, governing law, dispute and
consumer-rights language, age requirements, warranty/liability language, and
contact route after legal review.

## Community Rules — working draft

- Keep workout notes, comments, reactions, and photos relevant and respectful.
- Do not post harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, illegal content,
  impersonation, scams, or content that violates another person's rights.
- Do not share another person's private information or image without a proper
  basis.
- Use Report for content that needs moderator attention and Block to stop an
  unwanted member interaction.
- Support/moderation owners review reports through the agreed process. The
  founder must define response targets, escalation, emergency handling, and
  what happens to removed content before publication.
